# Mesh CoS v4.14.0 CxO Executive Risk Management

Release date: 2026-09-24

## Outcome

Adds a shared executive-risk decision-support contract and explicit risk remits to CRO, CFO, COO, and CMO without changing the 10-agent roster, TaskLedger authority, CxO functional truth ownership, or qualified-human approval boundaries.

## Material changes

- Adds `mesh.executive-risk.v1`.
- Adds CRO commercial-risk remit and cross-functional routing.
- Adds CFO economic-risk remit within Engagement Finance and management FP&A.
- Adds COO operational-risk remit with capacity evaluated only when concrete delivery need makes it decision relevant.
- Adds CMO brand, reputation, demand, channel, and public-narrative risk remit without publication authority.
- Adds executable BDD and contract validation.
- Adds Mermaid architecture and targeted security review.

## Compatibility

This is a backward-compatible repository MINOR release. Canonical Phase 1 authority/runtime contract remains unchanged. No new agent principal, connector permission, external action, pricing approval, staffing authority, public publishing authority, or risk-acceptance authority is introduced.
