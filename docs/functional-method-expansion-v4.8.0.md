# Functional Method Expansion v4.8.0

## Purpose

v4.8.0 deepens the methods available to the existing Mesh Phase 1 organization without adding agents, authority, canonical state, MCP tools, or autonomous external action.

Repository capability release: `v4.8.0`  
Canonical Phase 1 authority/runtime contract: `4.0.0`, unchanged  
Production QNAP deployment: `4.4.0`, unchanged  
Registered agents: exactly `10`

## Capability changes

### Chief of Staff

- Fit-for-purpose deliberation classifier from single-functional analysis through human escalation.
- Independent cross-functional contributions before synthesis where practical.
- Explicit disagreement, confidence, and reversal evidence.
- Governed Base / Stress / Severe scenario composition with Mesh PPMD Bot.
- TaskLedger-native strategic work-graph alignment diagnostics.
- Evidence-led change-readiness orchestration without HR authority.

### AgentOps

- P50/P90 cycle time, active-work/wait split, approval delay, rework, handoffs, WIP concentration, bottleneck, and recurring dependency signals when evidence supports them.
- Queueing methods only for genuine queued work with supported assumptions.
- Distributional capacity signals such as P50/P90/P99 demand where mathematically appropriate.

### Answer & Decision Desk

- Permission-aware answerability states and source-health diagnostics.
- Canonical owner, freshness, orphan knowledge, terminology drift, contradiction, runbook success/failure, rollback, and escalation evidence.
- Remediation routes to the authoritative owner rather than silent policy rewriting.

### CRO

- Pricing-model and package comparison with model + range outputs.
- Discount policy and precedent-risk analysis.
- Mesh-owned deal review economics with CFO composition.
- Commit/best-case/pipeline-upside forecasting with visible assumptions and confidence.
- Partnership qualification and sourced-versus-influenced attribution.
- Channel economics with CFO evidence.
- RFP/RFI proof discipline with STRONG/PARTIAL/GAP and non-negotiable proof integrity.

### CFO

- Channel cost-to-serve, discount economics, partner economics, supplier concentration, switching-cost/consolidation business cases, and commercial forecast cross-checks.
- Deterministic fixed-cost-to-serve deal discount calculation corrects the donor deal-desk formula defect.
- Substantial quantitative work continues to route through governed Mesh Data Analytics where appropriate.

### COO and Consultant Network Steward

- Current-state-first process diagnostics, fit-for-purpose capacity analysis, vendor/partner dependency, resilience, and procurement-process evidence.
- Consultant capability criticality, concentration, rate/availability freshness, contracting readiness, reliability, fallback, and contingency evidence.
- No procurement or staffing commitment authority is added.

### CMO, VP Content, Message Operations

- CMO growth-model, channel-allocation, marketing-investment, acquisition-economics, capacity, and change-readiness methods.
- VP Content proof/inventory freshness, orphan asset, terminology drift, duplicate/stale IP, lineage, claim-support, reusable proof-point, and derivative traceability methods.
- Message Operations may consume approved change/campaign execution metadata while preserving exact approval, sender/recipient, consent, suppression, frequency, duplicate/thread, idempotency, rollback, receipt, and kill-switch controls.

## Shared Skills

- `mesh-ppmd-bot` owns reusable scenario stress methodology.
- Mesh Messaging owns reusable change-communications methodology through Executive Communications and Marketing Messaging, with Messaging Orchestrator routing and Message Operations execution controls.
- `mesh-data-analytics`, Revenue Intelligence, GTM Orchestrator, and Mesh Devil's Advocate remain unchanged because their governing behavior did not require modification.

## Explicit non-changes

- no eleventh agent;
- no donor operating system or donor memory architecture;
- no new canonical database or parallel task/OKR store;
- no donor prompt-level invocation protocol;
- no runtime identity or MCP allowlist change;
- no autonomous pricing, deal, procurement, staffing, publication, or send authority;
- no QNAP runtime deployment;
- no private chain-of-thought persistence.
