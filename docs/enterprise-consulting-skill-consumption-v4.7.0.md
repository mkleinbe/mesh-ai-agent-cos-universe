# Enterprise Consulting Skill Consumption v4.7.0

## Purpose

Bind the newly released Mesh enterprise consulting mechanics into the canonical ten-agent operating model without changing the registry's authority, source, tool, parentage, delegation, approval, TaskLedger, or external-action contracts.

This is a capability-consumption release, not an authority release.

## Agent consumption model

### Chief of Staff

Direct binding already exists for Mesh PPMD Bot and Mesh Devil's Advocate. CoS may use falsification-first hypothesis mechanics, decision-linked synthesis, decision memos, outcome-first meeting preparation, and artifact-first workshops. CoS continues to route canonical facts to their functional owners and separately verifies outcomes before `VERIFIED`.

### CRO

Existing bindings already cover Revenue Intelligence, Firm 360, Competitive Displacement, GTM Orchestrator, Buyer Psychology, Sales Messaging, Client Servicing Messaging, and Devil's Advocate. CRO consumes the upgraded behaviors through those Skills. No new direct PPMD or Executive Communications binding is added.

### CFO

No new direct Skill binding. CFO supplies the financial evidence layer, risk frame, assumptions, confidence, sensitivities, financial reversal conditions, and next financial test to decision packages prepared by an authorized owner. CFO authority remains L3 recommendation within supported source scope.

### COO

No new direct Skill binding. COO supplies delivery evidence to CoS/PPMD meetings and workshops, including locked/open decisions, capacity/dependency constraints, workstream outputs, owners, commitments, evidence gaps, and approval dependencies. No staffing or delivery authority changes.

### CMO

Existing `mesh-executive-communications` binding consumes Mesh Messaging v1.2.0 decision memo structure. PPMD synthesis may arrive through an authorized CoS or consulting workflow. Design critic findings are review inputs from Mesh Design System / Artifact Designer, never a transfer of design or publication authority.

### Answer & Decision Desk

No new direct Skill binding. The Desk may answer from an approved decision artifact only when requester access, authoritative evidence, and approval state support the answer. Draft hypotheses, draft memos, workshop outputs, and critic findings are not policy or precedent.

### Unchanged agents

AgentOps Controller, Consultant Network Steward, VP Content, and Message Operations receive no new consulting-method binding in v4.7.0. Their canonical responsibilities and authority remain unchanged.

## Authority invariants

- Exactly 10 registered agents.
- Existing canonical parentage unchanged.
- Existing direct Skill arrays unchanged.
- Existing decision-authority levels unchanged.
- Existing tool allowlists and MCP action surface unchanged.
- TaskLedger remains canonical for ownership and task state.
- `COMPLETED` remains distinct from `VERIFIED`.
- Revenue Intelligence remains canonical for commercial/account and stakeholder truth where designated.
- CFO remains canonical for supported financial analysis within its approved source scope.
- COO remains canonical for delivery feasibility/capacity truth.
- CMO retains marketing authority but no autonomous publishing authority.
- Message Operations remains the approved communications execution boundary.
- L4 and L5 human approval requirements remain unchanged.

## Negative controls

Skill capability must never be interpreted as agent authority. Specifically:

- hypothesis confidence is not approval or canonical fact;
- synthesis does not create policy;
- a stakeholder map does not prove purchase intent, budget, authority, or advocacy;
- a decision memo does not approve its own recommendation;
- a meeting agenda does not create a commitment;
- a workshop artifact does not approve staffing, scope, price, or delivery commitment;
- a critic readiness status does not authorize publication or release;
- a completed artifact does not make the underlying outcome verified;
- no Skill output authorizes autonomous external communication.

## Compatibility

The canonical Phase 1 authority/runtime contract remains `4.0.0`. Production QNAP remains `4.4.0`. This repository capability release requires no MCP tool, schema, credential, database, deployment, or connector change.
