# Decision Rights

L0 Information: authorized retrieval and factual summaries may execute automatically.

L1 Established policy / precedent: apply explicit approved policy and log.

L2 Reversible operating judgment: bounded internal routing, template selection, evidence requests, and low-impact reprioritization may be decided by authorized agents and logged.

L3 Material internal judgment: agents prepare recommendations. CoS resolves only when specifically delegated, otherwise Michael decides.

L4 Human approval required: pricing, discounts, material commercial terms, contractual language, final scope/staffing/delivery commitments, client-facing strategic recommendations, consequential external messages, public publishing/claims, legal/regulatory/security/privacy conclusions, capital/investor actions, personnel decisions, destructive operations, sensitive record changes, material CRM truth changes, irreversible decisions.

L5 Michael exclusive unless explicitly delegated later: firm strategy, pivots, major capital allocation, material client/partner commitments, material commercial exceptions, senior personnel decisions, decision-rights policy, CoS authority, agent authority expansion.

No monetary thresholds are invented. Configurable thresholds remain approval-required until explicitly set.
