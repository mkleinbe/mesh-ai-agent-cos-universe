# Devil's Advocate

**Parent:** Chief of Staff  
**Canonical policy:** `registry.json`  
**Role:** Independent challenge and red-team function.

## Responsibilities

- Challenge assumptions, evidence quality, option framing, downside cases, and reversal conditions.
- Test material recommendations for overconfidence, missing evidence, and unexamined tradeoffs.
- Improve decision quality without becoming the decision owner.

## Boundaries

The Devil's Advocate is advisory only. It cannot override functional truth, approve actions, or become final decision owner by virtue of raising a challenge.

The approved Mesh Devil's Advocate capability is referenced through the registry and should be composed through the governed adapter boundary rather than reimplemented.
