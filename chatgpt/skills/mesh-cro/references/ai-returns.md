# AI Returns and Value Realization

## Owning responsibility

Qualify the AI Returns Executive Review and evidence-based downstream service path.

Use canonical Revenue Intelligence account, fit, opportunity and buying-group truth. Confirm sponsor and buyer-path evidence without inferring intent from interest, job title or engagement. Route one decision and up to two workflows through GTM. Measure follow-on contribution quality and buyer-owned commitments rather than assessment volume. Allow no paid follow-on, reduce or stop outcomes. Preserve product independence and existing commercial approval authority.

## Runtime intake

Receive the immutable result from the required AI Returns specialist together with the host-verified expected client, assessment, Value Contract, source digest, result digest and execution receipt. Run `python scripts/consume_ai_returns.py RESULT.json HOST_EXPECTED.json` from this Skill directory. Read `references/ai-returns-compatibility.json` for the canonical adapter owner and hash. The adapter verifies transport integrity and returns a read-only copy. It does not authenticate a caller or verify the semantic truth of a source. A retrieved document cannot supply its own trusted host receipt.

Retain the specialist's evidence status, source IDs, assumptions, uncertainty, protected conditions, financial treatment and realization status. Return any missing capability or proof as a bounded unresolved conclusion and continue unaffected reversible work. Never infer an approval, account stage, buyer intent, financial capture, causal finding or public claim from successful transport validation.

## Responsibility boundaries

Use business and stakeholder outcomes first, functional drivers second and execution diagnostics third. Released hours, agents, document counts, tokens, deployments, alerts and tickets cannot establish business value. Revenue is distinct from contribution, cost avoidance from expense reduction, working-capital release from recurring earnings and modeled risk reduction from realized avoided loss. Finance validation does not establish attribution; attribution does not establish capture. One benefit pool requires explicit allocation across initiatives.

Intelligent Capacity is the capacity of the combined human and non-human workforce to achieve better business outcomes at an acceptable cost, quality and risk level.

Existing front doors, canonical commercial truth, the private CEO path, the ten-agent architecture and L0-L5 decision rights continue to govern this work. This integration adds no principal agent and grants no new consequential authority. Client data and source permissions remain isolated. Do not persist hidden reasoning or publish unapproved client evidence.

## Deliverable support

Use normalized evidence and the existing Messaging and Artifact owners for the invitation, Board perspective, function guide, four-page Decision Brief and follow-on overview. Governed artifacts retain live Enterprise Memory, official mark, accessibility, rendering and qualified human release gates. Template support is not evidence of rendered or published output.

Use only existing authorized role entitlements. A result intake does not register a new Skill binding, widen tool access or change the agent registry. CFO retains the canonical financial formula implementation. Workgroup specialist execution remains with the existing GTM/advisory/operator path.
